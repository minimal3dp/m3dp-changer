# Reference geometry and validation inputs

The original duct comes from [cx330-TXY/CxChanger](https://github.com/cx330-TXY/CxChanger), corrected 6.11 assembly `打印头6.11修正.STEP`, product definition 330402 (`风道改`), assembly occurrence 80 in the extraction report. The source repository includes GPLv3; its license is retained with this bundle.

- `source/cx/original-4020-duct.step` is the extracted part-local source. The builder places it into assembly coordinates as `(x−0.05, 50.7−z, 25+y)` before selecting the main solid and modifying its inlet. The original two nozzle jets are retained.
- `source/cx/original-4020-duct-assembly-reference.brep` preserves that original assembly occurrence, including its source placement precision. It is used only to derive temporary caps for the independent enclosed-airway test. The validator selects its main solid; the small original intake membrane is not part of the replacement duct.
- The replacement adapter/carrier and side-fed duct modifications were authored for this integration. The editable native tree and generator retain the original duct as a separate source feature.

The four outer TAP attachment-hole positions are based on the [Chaoticlab CNC TAP V2 guide](https://github.com/Chaoticlab/CNC-Tap-for-Voron/blob/951225ebef3c1753b8fc599a90948c430e2c5943/Manual/CNC_VORON_TAP_V2_BUILD_GUIDE_20240222.pdf). Clearance investigation used the user's [Techne V2 measured reconstruction](https://www.printables.com/model/1853271-cnc-voron-tap-v2-unofficial-cad-reconstruction-ste), whose internal details are approximate. That third-party body and its assembly preview are not redistributed in this bundle. Physical TAP fit and travel remain required checks.

The independent air validator operates on the **actual exported duct BREP and STEP**, not merely the preflight model. Each must produce exactly two positive, disjoint air solids after temporary inlet/jet caps, with one enclosed cavity containing the neck, side port, original plenum and both jets. Exact input hashes are recorded in `top-export-airway-validation.json`; the native verifier checks those hashes after proving native-to-STEP surface agreement. FreeCAD's cap-union diagnostic was unreliable for this trimmed loft, so the air test is explicitly performed with PythonOCC, using coplanar-face refinement and a 0.000001 mm Boolean tolerance. No printed geometry is changed by those test operations.
